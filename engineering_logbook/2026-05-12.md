### AUTOSAR_E2E_comparison.md — E2E Protocol Mapping

Feature-by-feature mapping of UDP Q&A against AUTOSAR E2EProfile01 and WdgM.

Key findings:
- CRC-16/CCITT-FALSE provides stronger HD than E2EProfile01's CRC8 for comparable payloads
- uint8 counter equivalent to E2EProfile01 counter; stricter delta=1
- Timing window supervision maps to AUTOSAR WdgM alive supervision (beyond standard E2E)
- Challenge-response Q&A maps to external watchdog IC pattern (MAX6369 style)
- 7-step production upgrade path to full AUTOSAR compliance documented

### FTA.md — Fault Tree Analysis

ISO 26262-9 Clause 7 inspired methodology. Two fault trees with Mermaid diagrams.

**FTA-1** (Robot at unsafe speed — ASIL B):
- AND gate: software path AND hardware e-stop must both fail
- Dominant MCS: GPIO 25 wire break AND driver stuck HIGH (confirms FMEA SPOFs)
- Camera distance fusion reduces MCS-3 probability (dual distance sensor coverage)

**FTA-2** (Silent supervisor failure — ASIL A):
- Flow check gate closes silent-hang failure mode (BE16)
- Same GPIO 25 SPOFs appear as dominant cut sets
- Safety barrier diagram: dual independent paths proven effective

Cross-cutting: CCF analysis, dependent failure pairs, 5 ranked production recommendations.
